② Steps – tick them as you go

Your ticks are saved on this device only.

  1. 1
    Open your group sheet 📄Google Classroom → your group post
    Read the purpose, components, wiring diagram and code. Bring it to every lesson (printed or on iPad).
  2. 2
    Tinkercad first 💻Build and simulate the circuit in Tinkercad (checkpoint only). Use the substitutes on your sheet for parts Tinkercad does not have.
  3. 3
    Real build in class 🔌USB unplugged. Rails first (5V red, GND blue), then each part. A partner checks every wire before you plug in.
  4. 4
    Upload and calibrate 🎚️Arduino IDE → Board: Arduino Uno → Port → Upload. Serial Monitor 9600. Put YOUR numbers in the yellow CALIBRATE lines.
  5. 5
    Use AI and log it 🤖Use ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini / Z.ai to understand the code, plan calibration or improve the project. Copy prompts + your sensor data into the AI log.
  6. 6
    Test and improve 🧪Record readings in the table. Make one improvement and test again – before / after is your evidence.
  7. 7
    Cover + poster 🖼️Cardboard / stationery cover (idea on your sheet) and a clear A3 or Canva poster.
  8. 8
    Video 🎥1–2 minutes: the problem, the wiring, one AI prompt you used, a live demo and your results.
  9. 9
    Submit by Friday 30 October ⏰Poster, AI log, readings, video and the working project.

③ AI log – example prompts

Copy, change the [brackets], and write each prompt + answer in your AI log.

⑤ If something goes wrong

Upload fails

Tools › Port – choose the right COM port; close the Serial Monitor; try another USB cable.

LED does not light

Long leg to the pin through 220 Ω, short leg to GND rail. Check the LED is not backwards.

7-segment shows nothing

It may be common ANODE: COM to 5V and set SEG_COMMON_ANODE = true.

RGB colours wrong

Common anode: longest leg to 5V and set RGB_COMMON_ANODE = true.

Sensor reads nonsense

Check 5V, GND and the signal pin. Calibrate with YOUR readings.

DHT11 says nan

Install the Adafruit DHT library; add a 10k from DATA to 5V; wait 2 s between reads.

Button does nothing

It must straddle the centre gap; use legs on opposite sides.

🪜 My steps

  1. 💡1. Pick an idea – open one below.
  2. 📸2. Collect 30+ pictures for each class + a “Nothing” class.
  3. 🧠3. Train on Teachable Machine → Image Project → add classes → Upload → Train.
  4. 🧪4. Test & improve – try 10 times, count right answers, add more pictures, train again.
  5. 🌐5. Make it an app – Export → Upload my model → copy the link → build your page (see below).
  6. 🖼️6. Poster – name of my AI, what it recognises, who it helps, a screenshot, my full name – Grade 7-section.
  7. 🎥7. Video (1–2 min) – say your full name and class, how your AI learns, and your results.
  8. ✅8. Hand in the app link, poster and video on Google Classroom.

🌐 Turn your AI into an app

After training, press Export Model → Upload my model and copy the link. Then choose ONE way:

⭐ Way 1 – Base44, Lovable or Bolt.new (AI builds the page for you)

  1. Open Base44, Lovable or Bolt.new – with your teacher’s account.
  2. Copy the message below, change the [brackets], paste it and press Enter.
  3. Wait for the page. Press Allow for the camera and test it with your objects.
  4. Want a change? Ask in one short sentence, e.g. “make the letters bigger”.
  5. Press Publish / Share and copy your app link.
Make a simple, colourful web page for kids called "[name of my AI]".
Use my Teachable Machine image model from this link: [paste my model link].
Turn on the webcam with a big START button, show the camera picture,
and show what the AI sees in BIG letters with its percentage.
Add one helpful message for each class: [class 1] = "[message]", [class 2] = "[message]".
At the bottom write: Made by [my full name] – Grade 7-[section] – The First Academy.

🌐 Way 2 – Netlify Drop (publish it yourself)

  1. In Teachable Machine: Export Model → Tensorflow.js → copy the JavaScript code.
  2. Paste the code into a file called index.html (Notepad → Save as → All files). Add your full name and class at the bottom.
  3. Put index.html in a folder and drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop.
  4. Copy your new link and open it on a phone or iPad.

🔗 Way 3 – The quick link

  1. The link from “Upload my model” already opens a test page of your AI – use it if the app is not ready.
🛡️ Camera not working? Open your app link in Chrome and press Allow. Use free photo sites or your own photos – never photograph other people without asking.

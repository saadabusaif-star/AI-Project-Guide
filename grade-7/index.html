🪜 My steps

  1. 💡1. Pick an idea – open one below.
  2. 📸2. Collect 30+ pictures (or sounds) for each class. Add a “Nothing” class.
  3. 🧠3. Train on Teachable Machine.
  4. 🧪4. Test & improve – try 10 times, count right answers, add more samples, test again.
  5. 🖼️5. Poster – my name, what my AI recognises, who it helps, a screenshot.
  6. 🎥6. Video (1–2 min) – how my AI learns and my results.
  7. ✅7. Hand in on Google Classroom.
